## Next Primary Battle
The technical questions “can local code generate sophisticated MG?” and “can it learn bounded style preference?” are closed for core plumbing. The next battle is **real taste quality**:
1. collect real blind-reviewer votes and real timeline overrides at useful sample size;
2. prove preference lift on held-out real projects, not synthetic fixtures;
3. improve scene composition, transitions and per-effect editing without increasing decorative effect density.

## Constraints
- Renderer/provider never owns narrative meaning.
- Art Direction may restyle semantics, never rewrite them.
- No template-ID architecture or paid-provider release dependency.
- Do not animate every sentence; restraint is first-class.
- No provider-specific APIs in Motion IR.
- Render success ≠ semantic quality ≠ human preference.
- Preference learning must be evidence-based, scoped, bounded, versioned and reversible.
- Explicit user/brand constraints always outrank learned preference and heuristics.
